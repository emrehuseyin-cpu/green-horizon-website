Project Partners

Three complementary organisations from Germany, Türkiye, and Romania uniting expertise in European project management, digital youth education, and community climate action.

Project Coordinator (Applicant)

OID: E10325233

Bonn, Germany

GPS: 50.7374°N, 7.0982°E

P1 // COORDINATOR GERMANY (DE)

Integrational e.V.

Integrational e.V. is a German non-profit, non-governmental organisation based in Bonn, Germany. The organisation focuses on youth empowerment, digital literacy, and social inclusion, with a strong emphasis on climate advocacy through cutting-edge technology, digital media, and civic participation. As project coordinator, Integrational e.V. oversees overall project governance, quality assurance, and financial monitoring under grant agreement with the German National Agency (DE04 - JUGEND für Europa).

Key Responsibilities in Green Horizon:

  • Overall project management, day-to-day administration, and quality control (Activity A1).
  • Hosting the Kick-Off Transnational Project Meeting (TPM 1) in Bonn, Germany (13–14 June 2025).
  • Leading the project website development, maintenance, and long-term hosting (Activity A6).
  • Conducting German local workshops and aerial Rhine basin field missions with at least 20 youth participants (Activity A4).
  • Supervision of quarterly financial statements, timesheets, and official reporting to DE04 - JUGEND für Europa.
Contact Coordinator → info@greenhorizonyou.eu
Project Partner (Education & Climate Lead)

OID: E10315828

Mersin, Türkiye

GPS: 36.8121°N, 34.6415°E

P2 // EDUCATION LEAD TÜRKIYE (TR)

Yenilikçi Eğitim Bilim ve Sanat Derneği (IESA)

Yenilikçi Eğitim Bilim ve Sanat Derneği (Innovative Education, Science and Art Association) is a non-governmental organisation established in Mersin, Türkiye by educators, academics, artists, and entrepreneurs. IESA specializes in citizen participation, STEAM methodologies, digital youth work, and grassroots climate advocacy. In Green Horizon, IESA authors the educational booklet, acts as internal evaluator, and hosts the Final Transnational Project Meeting in Türkiye.

Key Responsibilities in Green Horizon:

  • Lead author for the Booklet on Drone Operation, Photography, and Effective Ways to Mitigate Climate Change (Activity A3).
  • Design of pedagogical modules, flight simulator drills, and ecological observation checklists.
  • Acting as internal project evaluator, ensuring quality control and methodology transfer.
  • Organizing Turkish local youth workshops and Lake Tuz/Mersin aerial documentation with 20 youth participants (Activity A4).
  • Hosting the Final Transnational Project Meeting (TPM 2) and Final Dissemination Conference in Türkiye (Activity A7).

Project Partner (Training Course & VR Lead)

OID: E10309212

Bucharest / Brașov, Romania

GPS: 44.4268°N, 26.1025°E

P3 // TRAINING & VR LEAD ROMANIA (RO)

Asociația Empower Plus

Asociația Empower Plus is a Romanian non-governmental youth organisation operating in Bucharest and Brașov, committed to non-formal education, digital literacy, and environmental empowerment. Empower Plus contributes deep expertise in audiovisual production, drone flight instruction, and interactive virtual reality exhibitions, connecting youth with community-driven ecological advocacy.

Key Responsibilities in Green Horizon:

  • Developing and hosting the Transnational Training Course for 18 Youth Workers in Brașov, Romania (Activity A2, 11–16 August 2025).

  • Curating and publishing the 360° Virtual Reality (VR) Exhibition on the Artsteps platform (Activity A5).
  • Conducting Romanian local youth workshops and Carpathian mountain forest canopy surveys with 20 youth (Activity A4).
  • Organizing Romanian local dissemination activities and community climate screenings.

Governance & Quality

Cooperation Arrangements & Quality Assurance

How the consortium ensures seamless coordination, transparency, and timely delivery across the 16-month lifecycle:

Decision-Making Structure

A Steering Committee comprising one designated representative per partner meets monthly online. Strategic decisions are taken by consensus, with formal voting (one vote per partner) if needed.

Communication Protocols

Bi-weekly operational syncs, shared cloud document storage for real-time output drafting, instant messaging for urgent field coordination, and structured email correspondence.

Risk & Conflict Management

Proactive dispute escalation ladder: informal bilateral dialogue → Steering Committee mediation → binding coordinator resolution in consultation with the German National Agency.

Budget & Time Oversight

Quarterly budget tracking versus the 16-month Gantt roadmap, time-sheeting, and interim milestone verification before grant tranche disbursements.

Erasmus+ KA210-YOU Project (1 May 2025 – 31 August 2026).
