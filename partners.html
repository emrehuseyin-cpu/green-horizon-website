Project Partners

Three complementary organisations from Germany, Türkiye, and Romania uniting expertise in European project management, digital youth education, and community climate action.

Project Coordinator

Bonn / Berlin, Germany

GPS: 50.7374°N, 7.0982°E

Integrational e.V.

Integrational e.V. is a German non-profit organisation committed to promoting European civic values, sustainable youth empowerment, social integration, and environmentally sound digital youth work. With longstanding experience in Erasmus+ cooperation partnerships and non-formal education schemes, Integrational e.V. brings extensive expertise in project governance, financial monitoring, and intercultural facilitation.

Key Responsibilities in Green Horizon:

  • Overall project governance, quality assurance, and European Commission reporting.
  • Organisation of the Kick-Off Transnational Project Meeting (TPM 1) in Bonn.
  • Leading the Transnational Drone Operation, Photography & Video Editing Training Course (A2).
  • Coordination of the monitoring framework, internal evaluation, and risk mitigation protocols.
  • Hosting German local workshops and aerial Rhine basin field missions.
Contact Coordinator → info@greenhorizonyou.eu
Project Partner

Ankara / Istanbul, Türkiye

GPS: 39.9334°N, 32.8597°E

Yenilikçi Eğitim Bilim ve Sanat Derneği (IESA)

Yenilikçi Eğitim Bilim ve Sanat Derneği (Innovative Education, Science and Art Association) is a progressive civil society organisation focused on STEAM learning methodologies, digital youth education, and creative media initiatives. IESA specializes in bridging emerging technologies—including robotic systems, UAVs, and digital arts—with youth-led environmental initiatives and critical media education.

Key Responsibilities in Green Horizon:

  • Lead developer of the Green Horizon Educational Booklet: Drones & Climate Storytelling (A3).
  • Design of pedagogical modules, flight simulator challenges, and field safety checklists.
  • Coordination of the local workshops and aerial documentation missions in Central Anatolia and Lake Tuz.
  • Technical mentoring in aerial cinematography, drone sensor calibration, and video post-production.
Contact IESA Team → iesa@greenhorizonyou.eu
Project Partner

Craiova / Bucharest, Romania

GPS: 44.3302°N, 23.7949°E

Asociația Empower Plus

Asociația Empower Plus is a non-governmental youth organisation from Romania focused on community development, social inclusion, environmental activism, and experiential non-formal learning. Empower Plus has active grassroots networks across Romanian youth clubs and is widely recognized for organizing inclusive youth cultural events, digital arts workshops, and ecological community campaigns.

Key Responsibilities in Green Horizon:

  • Lead partner for the Hybrid Photo & 360° Virtual Reality (VR) Exhibition (A6).
  • Design and technical curation of the interactive 360-degree web panorama exhibition portal.
  • Implementation of Romanian regional youth workshops and Carpathian mountain canopy surveys.
  • Hosting the Final Transnational Project Meeting (TPM 2) and European Dissemination Conference in Craiova.
Contact Empower Plus → empower@greenhorizonyou.eu

Erasmus+ KA210-YOU Project (1 May 2025 – 31 August 2026).
