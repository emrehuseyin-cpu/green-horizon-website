Project Partners

Three youth organisations from Germany, Türkiye, and Romania bringing together expertise in non-formal education, camera drone technology, and community climate action.

Project Coordinator

OID: E10325233

Bonn, Germany 🇩🇪

Integrational e.V.

Integrational e.V. is a German non-profit organisation based in Bonn. The organisation focuses on youth empowerment, digital literacy, and social inclusion, with a strong commitment to climate advocacy through accessible digital media and civic participation. As project coordinator, Integrational e.V. oversaw overall governance, quality assurance, financial administration, and web platform maintenance.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Overall project management, day-to-day coordination, and quality control (Activity A1).
  • Hosting the Kick-Off Transnational Project Meeting in Bonn, Germany (13–14 June 2025).
  • Leading the project website development, maintenance, and open-access hosting (Activity A6).
  • Conducting German local workshops with 20 youth participants in Bonn (Activity A4).
  • Administering financial reporting to the German National Agency (DE04 - JUGEND für Europa).

Partner (Education Lead)

OID: E10315828

Mersin, Türkiye 🇹🇷

Yenilikçi Eğitim Bilim ve Sanat Derneği (IESA)

Yenilikçi Eğitim Bilim ve Sanat Derneği (Innovative Education, Science and Art Association) is a non-governmental organisation established in Mersin, Türkiye by educators, artists, and innovators. IESA specializes in STEAM methodologies, citizen science, and grassroots youth advocacy. In Green Horizon, IESA authored the 99-page Educational Booklet, led internal evaluation, and hosted the Final Dissemination Conference in Mersin.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Lead author for the 99-page Green Horizon Educational Booklet (Activity A3).
  • Design of pedagogical curricula, camera drone flight practice drills, and safety guidelines.
  • Internal quality evaluator and methodology transfer specialist.
  • Conducting Turkish local workshops and aerial documentation with 20 youth in Mersin (Activity A4).
  • Hosting the Final Transnational Project Meeting and Dissemination Conference in Türkiye (Activity A7).

Partner (Training & VR Lead)

OID: E10309212

Bucharest / Brașov, Romania 🇷🇴

Asociația Empower Plus

Asociația Empower Plus is a Romanian youth organisation operating in Bucharest and Brașov, focused on empowering young people through non-formal education, audiovisual creativity, environmental awareness, and digital skills. With practical experience in camera drone instruction and digital arts, Empower Plus delivered the Transnational Training Course in Brașov and curated the 360° Virtual Reality exhibition.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Designing and delivering the Transnational Training Course for 18 Youth Workers in Brașov (Activity A2, 11–16 August 2025).
  • Curating the 360° Virtual Reality Exhibition on the Artsteps platform (Activity A5).
  • Technical mentoring for youth video editing and documentary post-production.
  • Conducting Romanian local workshops with 20 youth participants (Activity A4).
  • Organizing Romanian dissemination events and community screenings.
