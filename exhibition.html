Activity A5 • Virtual Reality

Climate Photo & 360° VR Exhibition

An immersive virtual walkthrough curated on the Artsteps platform, pairing high-resolution aerial and ground photography with youth reflections from Germany, Romania, and Türkiye.

Artsteps 3D Virtual Gallery Slot (Activity A5)

360° Virtual Reality Exhibition Room

Curated under the leadership of Asociația Empower Plus, the exhibition presents youth photography, aerial landscape captures, and environmental audio notes in a walk-through 3D gallery.

🚪 Enter Artsteps Exhibition → 📸 Browse 2D Photo Gallery

Curatorial Concept

The exhibition is arranged into three thematic rooms mirroring the handbook: (1) Climate Urgency; (2) Photography & Composition; (3) Aerial Drone Perspectives. Each room includes guided virtual audio notes.

Interactive Guided Tours

Visitors can follow a self-guided walkthrough or use automated camera waypoints to inspect high-resolution photographs, contextual captions, and location notes from partner cities.

Accessible Anywhere

Runs directly inside standard modern web browsers on laptops, tablets, and smartphones without requiring proprietary VR headsets, making it ideal for classroom and youth center screenings.
