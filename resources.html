Educational Resources & Toolkits

Freely accessible handbooks, drone safety protocols, facilitator toolkits, and non-formal curricular modules developed for European youth workers, schools, and climate advocates.

OFFICIAL PUBLICATION RELEASE Project Code: 2024-3-DE04-KA210-YOU-000293009

Booklet: Green Horizon — Youth Voices for Climate Action Through Drone Technology

The complete 99-page open-access manual created jointly by Integrational e.V. (Germany), Yenilikçi Eğitim Bilim ve Sanat Derneği (Türkiye), and Asociatia Empower Plus (Romania). Combines scientific climate mitigation, photographic narrative ethics, and hands-on drone flight curriculum with EU & Turkish aviation rules.

Curriculum Overview

Explore the 3 Core Booklet Chapters

Developed for youth workers, educators, and young people aged 15–30 to turn aerial observations into concrete climate action.

Chapter I // Pages 9–40

Climate Change Mitigation Strategies

Conceptual foundations of global climate change and its local manifestations. Details 5 observable environmental indicators: deforestation, coastal erosion, urban heat islands, water scarcity, and biodiversity loss. Explains community-based participatory observation, citizen science, and youth-led climate governance.

  • 5 Environmental indicators for local monitoring
  • Sustainable consumption & circular economy
  • Empowering youth as community changemakers
Read Chapter I (PDF p.9) →
Chapter II // Pages 41–72

Photography for Climate Storytelling

Foundations of photographic composition (rule of thirds, framing, leading lines, depth layering, lighting). Ethical visual representation and GDPR/KVKK consent. Framework for 4-stage climate narratives, combining aerial and ground perspectives, and curating exhibitions in physical spaces and 360° VR on Artsteps.

  • Principles of light, exposure, and composition
  • Single-image, series, and comparative storytelling
  • Exhibition curation & 360° VR dissemination
Read Chapter II (PDF p.41) →
Chapter III // Pages 73–98

Drone Operation for Documentation

Complete flight guide: multirotor anatomy, controller Mode 2, and flight modes. European (EASA Regulation 2019/947) and Turkish (SHGM SHT-İHA July 2026) aviation rules. 6 step-by-step flight training exercises, 7 cinematic shot types, repeat photography for change over time, and a 9-step video editing workflow.

  • 120m AGL ceiling, VLOS, and airspace checks
  • 6 Core flight training exercises for youth
  • 7 Cinematic aerial shots & video post-production
