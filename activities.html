Work Plan 2025–2026

Project Activities & Timeline

All seven planned Erasmus+ work packages (A1–A7) were successfully implemented and are marked as Completed (1 May 2025 – 31 August 2026).

Erasmus+ KA210-YOU • Official Roadmap

Chronological Timeline of Completed Activities

A record of all project mobilities, training sessions, local youth workshops, publications, and final conferences completed across Germany, Romania, and Türkiye.

1
Completed

Kick-off Meeting

📍 Germany • 👥 Participants: [number of participants]
28–29 July 2025

Project coordinators, communication leads, and youth workers met in Germany to launch the Green Horizon partnership. The consortium aligned on work packages, established quality and risk monitoring protocols, and agreed on the training course curriculum.

2
Completed

Training Course on Drone Operation, Photography and Video Editing

📍 Brașov, Romania • 👥 Participants: 18 youth workers from 3 countries
11–18 October 2025

An intensive 8-day training course hosted by Asociația Empower Plus bringing together 18 youth workers from Germany, Romania, and Türkiye. Participants received hands-on training in camera drone flight safety, photographic framing, and video post-production to act as multipliers.

3
Completed

Booklet on Drone Operation, Photography and Effective Ways to Mitigate Climate Change

📍 Digital Publication (Open Access) • 👥 Participants: Project Consortium
[publication date – to be added]

A comprehensive 99-page educational guide co-authored by the partnership covering climate change mitigation concepts, photography composition, and drone operation safety rules. Includes 6 practical flight drills and a printable field checklist.

4
Completed

Local Workshop

📍 Mersin, Türkiye • 👥 Participants: [number of participants]
19 April 2026

Organized by IESA, trained youth workers guided local youth in camera drone flight basics, environmental observation, and photographic composition. Participants documented local coastal and urban ecological topics.

5
Completed

Local Workshop

📍 Germany • 👥 Participants: [number of participants]
23 April 2026

Hosted by Integrational e.V., local young people learned camera drone safety, flight controls, and visual storytelling methods to observe riverbank habitats and urban green spaces.

6
Completed

Local Workshop

📍 Romania • 👥 Participants: [number of participants]
29 April 2026

Organized by Asociația Empower Plus, young participants practiced camera drone piloting, video capture, and photo framing to document local environmental realities.

7
Completed

Documentary Series Production & Exhibition Photo Selection

📍 Germany, Romania, and Türkiye • 👥 Participants: [number of participants]
May–July 2026

Young people worked in local production teams to film, edit, and finalize six short climate documentaries and curate high-resolution drone photography for the dissemination conferences and exhibition.

8
Completed

Dissemination Conference

📍 Romania • 👥 Participants: [number of participants]
17 July 2026

Asociația Empower Plus hosted the national dissemination event where youth-created documentary films and the Green Horizon photo exhibition were presented to local community members, educators, and stakeholders.

9
Completed

Dissemination Conference

📍 Mersin, Türkiye • 👥 Participants: [number of participants]
22 July 2026

IESA organized the dissemination conference in Mersin, showcasing the project results, screening the documentary short films, and exhibiting photographs of local climate challenges to community stakeholders.

10
Completed

Dissemination Conference

📍 Germany • 👥 Participants: [number of participants]
25 July 2026

Integrational e.V. presented the final project results, screening the youth documentaries and displaying the photography collection to local youth leaders, educators, and community representatives.

11
Completed

Final Partner Meeting

📍 Mersin, Türkiye • 👥 Participants: [number of participants]
31 July – 1 August 2026

Representatives from Integrational e.V., Asociația Empower Plus, and IESA met in Mersin to evaluate all project results, review participant feedback, finalize reporting procedures, and plan future collaboration.

12
Completed

VR Exhibition Online Launch

📍 Online (MetaSteps / Artsteps Virtual Gallery) • 👥 Participants: Open to the public worldwide
October 2026

The digital version of the photo exhibitions presented at the conferences went live online in October 2026. It allows visitors worldwide to take a guided virtual tour of youth drone photography and explore climate change in Germany, Romania, and Türkiye from above.
