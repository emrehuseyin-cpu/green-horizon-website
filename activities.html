HOME // PROJECT WORK PLAN

Project Activities & Timeline

Structured according to the Erasmus+ project work plan (1 May 2025 – 31 August 2026). Activities are presented transparently with their official work package status.

ℹ

Implementation Protocol Notice: The milestones listed below reflect the approved Erasmus+ KA210-YOU project application and monitoring roadmap. Activity progress and deliverables are updated systematically as each implementation phase progresses.

Work Plan 2025–2026

Milestone Roadmap

Chronological overview from Month 1 (Kick-off) through Month 16 (Final Evaluation).

MONTH 1 // MAY 2025 Planned Work Package

A1: Project Governance & Kick-Off Meeting (TPM 1)

📍 Bonn, Germany • Host: Integrational e.V.

Inaugural meeting of consortium coordinators to sign partnership agreements, establish risk and quality management procedures, and calibrate the comprehensive monitoring matrix.

Key Output: Partnership Agreement & Quality Assurance Manual
MONTH 4–5 // AUG–SEP 2025 In Preparation

A2: Drone Operation & Video Editing Training Course

📍 Germany • Transnational Mobility

Transnational training mobility bringing together 15 youth workers from DE, TR, and RO for intensive theoretical and flight certification in EASA drone safety, aerial photography, and cinematic editing.

Key Output: Certified Youth Drone Trainers & Safety Protocols
MONTH 6–8 // OCT–DEC 2025 In Development

A3: Educational Booklet on Drones & Storytelling

📍 Digital Output • Lead: IESA (Türkiye)

Creation of an open-access pedagogical handbook for youth workers, containing 6 structured learning modules, pre-flight checklists, and non-formal lesson plans in English, German, Turkish, and Romanian.

Key Output: Open-Access PDF Handbook & Facilitator Kit
MONTH 8–11 // DEC 2025 – MAR 2026 Planned

A4: Three Local Youth Workshops & Field Missions

📍 Bonn (DE), Ankara/Konya (TR), Craiova (RO)

Local non-formal workshops engaging young participants in flight simulators, environmental dialogue, and aerial field documentation of regional ecological challenges.

Key Output: Grassroots Youth Engagement & Raw Aerial Repositories
MONTH 11–13 // MAR–MAY 2026 Planned

A5: Youth Climate Action Documentary Series

📍 Online Digital Production • Consortium-wide

Youth-directed mini-series combining cinematic drone perspectives and environmental journalism to shed light on European climate adaptation.

Key Output: Multi-Episode Video Series with Subtitles
MONTH 13–14 // MAY–JUN 2026 Planned

A6: Hybrid Climate Photo & 360° VR Exhibition

📍 Web Portal & Partner Venues • Lead: Empower Plus (Romania)

Interactive 360-degree virtual reality exhibition showcasing aerial panoramas, accompanied by printable high-resolution exhibition collections for local community centres.

Key Output: 360° Virtual Exhibition Portal & Exhibition Catalogue
MONTH 14–16 // JUN–AUG 2026 Planned

A7: Transnational Dissemination Conferences

📍 Bonn, Ankara, Craiova • All Partners

Public multiplier screenings, open roundtables with educational authorities, and digital campaigns presenting project deliverables to civil society and Erasmus+ stakeholders.

Key Output: Dissemination Assessment & Policy Brief
MONTH 16 // AUG 2026 Planned

A8: Final Transnational Project Meeting (TPM 2)

📍 Craiova, Romania • Host: Asociația Empower Plus

Consortium evaluation assembly to review final qualitative and quantitative impact metrics, complete the Erasmus+ Final Beneficiary Report, and establish post-project maintenance.

Key Output: Final Evaluation Report & Sustainability Agreement

Erasmus+ KA210-YOU Project (1 May 2025 – 31 August 2026).
