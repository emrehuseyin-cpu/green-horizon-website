Work Plan 2025–2026

Milestone Roadmap

Chronological overview from Month 1 (Kick-off) through Month 16 (Final Evaluation).

MONTH 1 // 13–14 JUNE 2025 Grant: €16,005

A1: Project Management & Kick-Off Meeting (TPM 1)

📍 Bonn, Germany • Host: Integrational e.V.

Inaugural meeting of consortium coordinators, communication specialists, and youth workers. Finalizes partnership agreements, monitoring matrix, risk management protocols, and quarterly timesheet systems.

Key Output: Consortium Agreements, M&E Framework, Quality Plan
MONTH 4 // 11–16 AUGUST 2025 Grant: €22,588

A2: Drone Operation, Photography & Video Editing Training Course

📍 Romania • Lead: Asociatia Empower Plus • 18 Youth Workers

6-day intensive training course bringing together 18 youth workers (6 DE, 6 TR, 6 RO) to master drone piloting, EASA flight regulations, aerial photography, and video editing to become certified multipliers.

Key Output: 18 Certified Drone Operators + Youthpass Certificates
MONTH 6 // 01–31 OCTOBER 2025 Grant: €3,000

A3: Booklet on Drone Operation, Photography & Climate Mitigation

📍 Digital Output • Lead: IESA (Türkiye)

Publication of the open-access handbook covering 3 core areas: technical drone operations, visual environmental storytelling, and actionable climate mitigation strategies with EN, DE, TR, RO translations.

Key Output: Open-Access PDF Booklet (>300 Downloads Target)
MONTH 8 // 15 DECEMBER 2025 Grant: €7,200

A4: 3 x Local Youth Workshops

📍 Bonn (DE), Mersin (TR), Bucharest/Brașov (RO) • 60 Youth Total

Trained youth workers lead 1-day workshops for at least 20 local youth per country (minimum 60 young people aged 15-30 total). Each partner co-finances a dedicated drone for flight simulations and field documentation.

Key Output: 60 Local Youth Trained in Drone Tech & Raw Aerial Media
MONTH 11 // 01–31 MARCH 2026 Grant: €3,000

A5: Documentary Series & VR Photo Exhibition on Artsteps

📍 Online & Artsteps Platform • Lead: Asociatia Empower Plus

Youth participants create at least 6 documentary short films (2–10 minutes each) and an interactive 360-degree VR photo exhibition on Artsteps showcasing environmental degradation and youth solutions.

Key Output: 6 Documentary Short Films & 360° Artsteps VR Exhibition
MONTH 5–16 // SEP 2025 – AUG 2026 Grant: €1,000

A6: Project Website Construction & Dissemination Hub

📍 https://greenhorizonyou.eu • Lead: Integrational e.V.

Development and maintenance of the central project platform for at least 3 years post-project. Monitored against target metrics: >3,000 total visitors and >300 booklet downloads under Open Access licensing.

Key Output: Accessible Digital Hub (WCAG 2.1 AA) & Open Access Repository
MONTH 12–16 // APR–AUG 2026 Grant: €7,207

A7: Dissemination Events & Final TPM in Türkiye

📍 Mersin / Ankara, Türkiye • Lead: IESA

Three national dissemination conferences with at least 20 participants per country (60+ total). Culminating with the Final Transnational Project Meeting in Türkiye to draft the Final Report and Sustainability Plan.

Key Output: Final Report, 3 Conferences, and Post-Project Sustainability Pact
Approved Dissemination Table

Official Dissemination Indicators & Targets

Metrics established in the proposal's Initial Dissemination Plan to measure public reach and European impact.

Website Reach

>3,000 Total Visits

Target: 150 monthly visitors across project lifetime. Website maintained for at least 3 years after project completion under Open Access.

Booklet Exploitation

>300 Booklet Downloads

Free open-access digital handbook downloads by youth workers, educators, schools, and environmental NGOs across Europe.

Social Media Community

>1,000 Social Followers

Active accounts across Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube managed by Integrational e.V. from Month 3 with interactive youth content.

Posts & Stories

>30 Stories & Posts

Minimum 10 social media stories and posts produced by each partner in national languages (DE, TR, RO) and English.

Audience Engagement

>150 Views per Post

At least 50 views per post per partner, engaging community stakeholders in climate awareness and digital youth work.

Mass Media & Press

9 Press Releases / Articles

Each partner publishes 3 press articles in national newspapers, digital journals, and magazines (18 multiplier reach).

Consortium Newsletters

3 Digital Newsletters

Distributed at Project Start (Introductory), Middle (post-workshops results), and Final phase (pre-dissemination conferences).

Positive Short Videos

6 Positive Videos

2 positive documentary micro-videos per partner highlighting youth changemakers, solutions, and project milestones.
