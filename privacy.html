Privacy Policy

Transparency and compliance under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Data Controller

The Data Controller responsible for data processing on this website (https://greenhorizonyou.eu) is:
Integrational e.V.
Project Coordinator for Green Horizon (Erasmus+ KA210-YOU)
Bonn / Berlin, Germany
Email: info@greenhorizonyou.eu

2. Web Hosting & Server Log Files

Our website is hosted on shared web infrastructure provided by HostGator. When accessing https://greenhorizonyou.eu, the web server automatically records temporary information in standard server log files, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system used
  • Referrer URL (the previously visited website)
  • Host name of the accessing terminal device
  • Date and time of server inquiry
  • IP address (anonymized/masked by server configuration)

The legal basis for this processing is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the technical stability, security, and integrity of the website). Server logs are retained temporarily for diagnostic purposes and are not linked to individual profiles.

3. Contact Form & Communications

When you send us inquiries via the contact form or email, the information you provide (name, email address, inquiry subject, message text, country) is processed exclusively for addressing and responding to your inquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contractual or pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to project communications). Data is not transferred to unauthorized third parties and is retained only as long as necessary to resolve the inquiry.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We adhere to strict data-minimization practices. This website uses only essential technical storage mechanisms:

  • gh_lang: Remembers your chosen display language (EN, DE, TR, RO) in browser localStorage.
  • gh_cookie_consent: Remembers your cookie acknowledgement preference.

We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site profiling trackers, or invasive analytics pixels.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject, you hold comprehensive rights under European data protection legislation:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR): Inquire about personal data processed by us.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restriction (Art. 18 GDPR): Request restriction of processing.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR): Receive your data in a structured format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR): Object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: info@greenhorizonyou.eu. You also retain the right to lodge a complaint with a competent European data protection supervisory authority.

6. Erasmus+ Funding & Project Accountability

Green Horizon is co-funded by the Erasmus+ Programme of the European Union. Project documentation, participant attendance lists, and financial audits are governed by European Commission grant rules and applicable statutory retention periods.
