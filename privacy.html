Privacy Policy

Information on data processing, cookie policies, and your rights under EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation EU 2016/679).

1. Data Controller

The data controller for this website (https://greenhorizonyou.eu) is Integrational e.V., based in Bonn, Germany, acting as the coordinating organisation of the Erasmus+ project Green Horizon: Youth Voices for Climate Action Through Drone Technology (Project Number: 2024-3-DE04-KA210-YOU-000293009). Contact: info@greenhorizonyou.eu.

2. Photographic Consent & Youth Privacy

All photographs, videos, and media featured on this website from transnational mobilities, training sessions, and workshops were published with the explicit informed consent of the participants shown (and their legal guardians for minor participants).

3. Technical Server Logs & Cookies

This website uses only essential technical cookies necessary for page delivery, navigation, and user interface preferences (such as checklist states and cookie consent preferences stored locally in your browser). We do not use third-party tracking or behavioral advertising cookies.

4. Contact Form Processing

When you send a message through our contact form, your name, email address, country, and message content are transmitted securely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell or transfer your personal contact information to third parties.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. You may also withdraw any consent previously given. To exercise your rights, please email info@greenhorizonyou.eu.
