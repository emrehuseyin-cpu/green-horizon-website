Project Context & European Dimension

Bridging Advanced Technology and Grassroots Climate Advocacy

Green Horizon is a 16-month European cooperation partnership connecting young people, educators, and youth workers from Germany, Türkiye, and Romania.

[01 // THE CHALLENGE]

Invisible Environmental Scars

Climate change impacts—from severe river droughts and receding inland lakes to high-altitude forest dieback—are often difficult to observe from ground level. Traditional advocacy often lacks immediate, tangible visual proof.

[02 // THE INNOVATION]

The Aerial Perspective

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) provide unprecedented non-invasive access to remote ecosystems. By training youth to pilot drones safely and interpret aerial imagery, we turn complex ecological data into compelling visual narratives.

[03 // THE EMPOWERMENT]

Youth as Environmental Reporters

Rather than passive recipients of climate anxiety, participating youth become certified drone operators, documentary storytellers, and climate ambassadors within their local communities and European networks.

Core Objectives

Our Strategic Focus Areas

Six interconnected pillars designed to produce sustainable pedagogical models and real community impact.

Climate Awareness

Investigating regional climate consequences and ecological adaptation across diverse European biomes.

Drone Technology

Mastering UAV flight principles, flight planning, sensor operation, and rigorous EASA safety regulations.

Digital & Eco Literacy

Equipping youth with data verification, critical media literacy, and non-invasive ecological monitoring skills.

Youth Participation & Inclusion

Active engagement of youth with fewer opportunities, breaking socioeconomic and geographical barriers.

Visual Storytelling

Producing cinematic short documentaries, social media reels, and high-impact environmental advocacy pieces.

Sustainable Toolkit

Developing freely accessible educational booklets, checklists, and 360° VR blueprints for non-formal educators.

Project Work Plan

Core Activities & Milestones

Work packages as defined in the Erasmus+ application (1 May 2025 – 31 August 2026).

A1 // M1 Planned

Kick-Off Meeting (TPM 1)

Consortium governance, quality management, and safety alignment in Bonn, Germany.

A2 // M4-M5 In Prep

Transnational Training Course

Intensive training in drone piloting, EASA flight regulations, and camera operations.

A3 // M6-M8 In Dev

Educational Booklet

Publication of 6-module curriculum for youth workers and environmental educators.

A4 // M8-M11 Planned

Three Local Youth Workshops

Hands-on flight labs and local ecological documentation in DE, TR, and RO.

A5 // M11-M13 Planned

Documentary Series

Youth-directed video episodes covering river basins, droughts, and forest canopies.

A6 // M13-M14 Planned

Photo & 360° VR Exhibition

Interactive virtual reality gallery and physical exhibition of aerial photography.
