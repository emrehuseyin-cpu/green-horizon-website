GREEN HORIZON: YOUTH VOICES FOR CLIMATE ACTION THROUGH DRONE TECHNOLOGY

An Erasmus+ youth partnership equipping young people and youth workers with practical skills in camera drone piloting, environmental photography, and visual storytelling to document climate challenges and drive community action.

Transnational Partnership

Where We Worked

Connecting youth across Germany, Romania, and Türkiye: Bonn (DE), Mersin (TR), and Brașov (RO).

Bonn, Germany 🇩🇪

Integrational e.V. — Project coordinator; VR photo exhibition, local workshops and dissemination.

Brașov, Romania 🇷🇴

Asociația Empower Plus — Training course host (A2) and documentary series.

Mersin, Türkiye 🇹🇷

IESA — Booklet lead; host of the Mersin dissemination conference and final meeting.
