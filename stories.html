Climate Stories From Above

Bridging aerial technology and environmental journalism. Explore regional case studies, video investigations, and ecological reflections produced by youth teams across Germany, Türkiye, and Romania.

Production Phase // Work Package A5

Youth Climate Action Documentary Series

Three youth-directed episodes exploring river basin low-waters in Germany, aridification in Anatolia, and virgin forest protection in Romania.

Field Mission Dossiers

Regional Climate Case Studies

GERMANY // CASE 01
GPS: 50°21'08"N, 7°35'45"E | ALT: 115M AGL

Waterlines in Flux: Aerial Hydrology of the Rhine Basin

Documenting extreme seasonal water level fluctuations along the Rhine River using drone orthomosaic imaging, examining climate resilience measures and alluvial wetland restoration.

KEY INVESTIGATION HIGHLIGHTS:
  • Orthomosaic mapping of receding gravel bars
  • Thermal infrared detection of riparian tree heat stress
  • Interviews with water management engineers
TÜRKIYE // CASE 02
GPS: 38°45'12"N, 33°20'18"E | ALT: 140M AGL

The Shrinking Salt Flat: Drone Perspectives on Central Anatolia

Exploring the rapid hydrological changes of Lake Tuz and the Konya Closed Basin through high-altitude aerial sweeps, highlighting flamingo nesting sanctuaries and agricultural groundwater depletion.

KEY INVESTIGATION HIGHLIGHTS:
  • High-contrast aerial textures of salt crusting
  • Flamingo breeding sanctuary perimeter surveillance
  • Youth advocacy dialogues on water governance
ROMANIA // CASE 03
GPS: 45°36'02"N, 24°37'52"E | ALT: 120M AGL

Canopy Guardians: Monitoring Virgin Forests in the Carpathians

Deploying aerial imaging across steep mountain valleys to monitor primeval beech forest canopies, bark beetle infestations, and intact wildlife migration corridors.

KEY INVESTIGATION HIGHLIGHTS:
  • Canopy crown density assessments via aerial sweeps
  • 360° VR panorama capture for the virtual exhibition
  • Comparative visual inventories of intact vs fragmented forests
